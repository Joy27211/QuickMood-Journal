# 🌙 QuickMood Journal

A simple, beautiful **daily mood tracker** web app that helps you log how you feel each day, write short notes, and track your journaling streak — all in your browser, no sign-up needed.

---

## ✨ Features

- **Mood Logging** — Choose from 5 moods: Amazing, Happy, Neutral, Sad, Awful
- **Daily Notes** — Write a short note about your day alongside your mood
- **Motivational Quotes** — Get a personalized quote based on your mood after saving
- **Journal History** — View all your past entries in a clean timeline
- **Streak Tracker** — See how many consecutive days you've logged your mood 🔥
- **Delete Entries** — Remove any entry you no longer want
- **Persistent Storage** — All data is saved in your browser's localStorage (no account needed)

## 💡 How It Works

1. **Select a mood** by clicking one of the emoji buttons
2. **Write an optional note** about your day
3. **Hit Save** — your entry is stored in `localStorage`
4. A **motivational quote** appears based on your mood
5. Scroll down to see your **full journal history**
6. Come back daily to build your **streak!** 🔥
